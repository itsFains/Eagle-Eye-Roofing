Stop losing money to Dirt & Debris.

Solar panels rely on direct sunlight to generate electricity. When a layer of traffic dust, thick pollen, algae, and bird droppings covers the glass, the sun simply cannot penetrate properly. Rainwater alone is not enough to wash off this stubborn, baked-on grime.

Furthermore, the warm gap between your solar panels and your roof tiles is the perfect nesting spot for pigeons and seagulls. Their highly acidic droppings damage the panel surface, while their nests can block ventilation and lead to chewed wiring. We solve both problems safely and efficiently.

    Boost Your Energy Yield

    Safe, Chemical-Free Cleaning

    We use specialist water-fed poles and purified, de-ionized water. No abrasive chemicals that could void your manufacturer's warranty.

Our Maintenance Services

PROTECT YOUR PANELS

◇

Pure Water Cleaning

We safely scrub your panels using soft-bristle brushes on telescopic poles, feeding up 100% purified water. This leaves the glass completely spotless with zero streaks or mineral residue to block the sun.

◇

Bird & Pigeon Proofing

We install specialized, galvanized steel mesh directly around the outer edge of your solar array. This creates an impenetrable barrier that stops birds from nesting underneath, protecting your wiring and your roof.

◇

Health Inspections

While we are up on the roof, our trained team will conduct a visual check of the solar mounting brackets, clamps, and underlying roof tiles to ensure everything remains secure and watertight.
