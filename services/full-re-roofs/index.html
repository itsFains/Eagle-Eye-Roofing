Why upgrade to a New Roof?

Your roof is the first line of defense for your property. Over decades, constant exposure to rain, high winds, and sun naturally degrades even the toughest slates and under-felt. If you are constantly paying for patch repairs, a full re-roof is often the most cost-effective and stress-free long-term solution.

At Eagle Eye Roofing & Building, we strip your roof back to the bare timbers. We assess the structural integrity, replace any rotten battens, install highly breathable modern membranes, and lay a brand-new roof covering that instantly boosts the value and kerb appeal of your home.

Is it time?

4 SIGNS YOU NEED A NEW ROOF

◇

Constant Leaks

If water is frequently breaching your ceiling or running down your interior walls, your under-felt has likely completely perished.

◇

Daylight in the Attic

Stand in your loft with the lights off. If you can see daylight piercing through the roof boards, rain and wind are definitely getting in.

◇

Sagging Roofline

A visible dip or curve in your roof ridge indicates that the timber joists underneath are rotting or struggling to hold the weight.

◇

Old Age

Most traditional tile roofs are built to last 40-50 years. If your home hasn't had a new roof since the 1980s, its lifespan is likely up.

How We Work

THE RE-ROOFING PROCESS

1

Scaffolding & Setup

We arrange for safe, compliant scaffolding to be erected around your property to protect our team and your home.

2

Stripping the Old Roof

We carefully strip off all existing tiles, slates, battens, and the perished felt underneath, disposing of all waste responsibly.

3

Membrane & Battens

We roll out a modern, highly breathable, waterproof membrane, followed by fixing brand-new, treated timber battens across the roof.

4

Laying the New Roof

Our expert roofers lay your chosen material—whether natural Welsh slate or interlocking concrete tiles—ensuring perfect alignment and complete weatherproofing.

5

Ridge & Leadwork Finish

We install a modern dry-ridge system for superior ventilation, meticulously dress any lead flashings around chimneys, and perform a final quality check.
