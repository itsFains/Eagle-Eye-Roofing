The Problem with Old Flat Roofs

Historically, flat roofs in the UK were covered using hot bitumen and traditional roofing felt. Over time, the Welsh sun bakes this felt until it becomes brittle, and the freezing rain cracks it open. The result? Persistent leaks that ruin garage ceilings and extension interiors.

At Eagle Eye Roofing & Building, we rip out these failed systems and replace them with high-tech, seamless materials that are incredibly resilient to extreme weather.

EPDM Rubber Roofing

Firestone EPDM is a synthetic rubber membrane that provides unmatched durability. It is laid in a single, seamless piece for most residential extensions, eliminating vulnerable joints where water usually penetrates.

  • Highly flexible to expand/contract with heat.
  • UV and ozone resistant.
  • Expected lifespan of up to 50 years.
  • Eco-friendly and fully recyclable.

GRP Fibreglass

Glass Reinforced Plastic (GRP) provides an incredibly tough, architectural finish. It sets completely rigid, forming a single continuous shell over your roof that is tough enough to withstand foot traffic.

  • Extremely tough and vandal-proof.
  • No joints, seams, or welds.
  • Sleek, modern architectural appearance.
  • Excellent for roofs acting as balconies.

Is it failing?

Signs your flat roof needs replacing

A failing flat roof rarely gets better on its own. Ignoring the early warning signs often leads to rotting timber decking, ruined insulation, and expensive internal re-plastering. Look out for:

Ponding Water

If large puddles of water remain on your roof 48 hours after rain, the decking has sagged and the roof is no longer draining properly.

Blistering & Cracking

Old felt often bubbles up (blistering) due to trapped moisture expanding in the sun, leading to cracks that let rain pour straight in.

Heavy Moss Growth

While some moss is normal, excessive growth means the roof is holding too much moisture, which acts like a sponge destroying the felt beneath.
