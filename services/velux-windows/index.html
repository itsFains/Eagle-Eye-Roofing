Velux Windows
& Skylights

Transform dark loft conversions and extensions into bright, airy living spaces. We install, replace, and repair premium Velux roof windows with guaranteed watertight flashing across South Wales.

Book a Free Survey ☎ 07753 865817

Let the light in. Keep the rain out.

A well-placed roof window can completely transform the feel of a room, bringing in up to twice as much daylight as vertical windows of the same size. However, the biggest hesitation homeowners have when considering a skylight is the fear of it leaking during heavy Welsh storms.

As professional roofers and leadwork specialists, Eagle Eye Roofing & Building eliminates that risk. We don't just cut a hole and drop a window in; we expertly integrate the Velux flashing kits directly into your existing slates or tiles, ensuring a 100% watertight, seamless finish that will last for decades.

  • ✓

    Superior Thermal Efficiency

    Modern Velux windows are highly insulated, preventing heat loss in the winter while allowing fresh ventilation during summer.

  • ✓

    Expert Flashing Installation

    Properly installed flashing is critical. Our roofing expertise means your window is sealed perfectly against the elements.

Our Velux Services

INSTALLATION & REPAIRS

◇

Brand New Installations

Whether you are building a new extension, converting your loft, or just want to brighten up a dark hallway, we can cut into your existing roof structure, frame out the opening securely, and install a brand-new Velux window from scratch.

◇

Upgrades & Replacements

Old skylights from the 80s and 90s are notorious for drafting and condensation. We can quickly swap out your old, blown window for a modern, double or triple-glazed Velux unit, vastly improving your home's thermal efficiency.

◇

Flashing & Leak Repairs

If water is dripping around the frame of your existing skylight, the surrounding flashing has likely failed. We can carefully strip back the surrounding tiles, replace the perished lead or aluminium flashing kit, and make the window watertight again.

◇

Flat Roof Skylights

It's not just pitched roofs! We also install highly secure, curved or flat-glass Velux domes onto EPDM and GRP flat roofs. These are perfect for modern kitchen extensions where natural light is a priority.
