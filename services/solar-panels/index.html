Traditional solar is bulky. We do better.

For years, the only way to get solar panels was to bolt heavy, ugly metal brackets straight through your roof tiles. These "bolt-on" systems are notorious for creating gaps where birds nest and wind can rip the tiles clean off.

As professional roofers, we believe your roof should look incredible while doing its job. That is why Eagle Eye Roofing installs the Marley SolarTile system. These panels are engineered to act as the actual roof covering. They sit completely flush and seamlessly interlock with your surrounding slates or tiles.

Hurricane Wind Proof

The certified wind resistance for Marley SolarTile is more than four times higher than competitor PV roof tiles. Because it sits flush with the roofline, high coastal winds simply pass over it rather than catching underneath.

Highest Fire Rating

It is the only roof-integrated solar system accredited with the highest resistance to spread of flame and fire penetration in all European fire safety tests, requiring no extra fireproofing material.

15-Year Guarantee

When installed by a professional roofer as part of a complete roof system, Marley SolarTiles are fully covered by an industry-leading 15-year guarantee for total peace of mind.
