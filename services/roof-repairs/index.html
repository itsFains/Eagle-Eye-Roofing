Stop the leak Right the first time.

Finding the true source of a roof leak requires an expert eye. Water can enter through a cracked slate near the chimney, travel down the underlying felt, and drip into your ceiling ten feet away from the actual hole.

At Eagle Eye Roofing, we don't just temporarily patch over the symptom; we diagnose and fix the core structural issue. Whether it's replacing a few blown slates after a heavy Welsh storm, re-bedding loose ridge tiles, or fixing perished lead flashing, we ensure your home is completely watertight.

WHAT WE FIX

◇

Storm Damage & Blown Tiles

High winds regularly dislodge slates and concrete tiles across South Wales. We quickly source matching replacements and securely nail them back into place to protect the exposed under-felt.

◇

Perished Lead Flashing

The leadwork around your chimney and roof valleys is the most common failure point for leaks. We carefully strip out split or degraded lead and weld in fresh, waterproof replacements.

◇

Loose Ridge Tiles

Over time, the mortar holding the triangular tiles at the very top of your roof cracks and crumbles. We chip out the old cement and re-bed them, or upgrade you to a modern dry-ridge system.

◇

Flat Roof Punctures

Traditional felt flat roofs frequently blister and crack in the sun. We provide rapid patch repairs or recommend overlaying the damage with seamless GRP or EPDM rubber.
