Don't ignore the Overflow.

Your gutters have one vital job: to safely carry heavy rainwater away from your home's roofline and foundations. When they become blocked with leaves, moss, and debris, that water has nowhere to go. It overflows, running directly down your brickwork.

Over time, this constant stream of water causes penetrating damp, ruins interior plastering, and can even cause structural shifting. At Eagle Eye Roofing, we offer comprehensive maintenance. Whether you just need a seasonal clear-out or your old, brittle plastic gutters replaced entirely, our team has you covered.

Our Gutter Services

MAINTENANCE & UPGRADES

◇

Gutter Clearing

We manually scoop out compacted mud, rotting leaves, and thick moss that clogs your system. We also unblock choked downpipes to ensure water flows freely into the drains.

◇

Leak Repairs

Dripping joints are the most common gutter issue. We strip out perished rubber seals, clean the connection points, and fit new seals or brackets to stop the dripping immediately.

◇

New Installations

If your old plastic gutters have gone brittle and sun-bleached, we replace them entirely. We install high-capacity, deep-flow UPVC systems designed to handle the heaviest Welsh storms.
