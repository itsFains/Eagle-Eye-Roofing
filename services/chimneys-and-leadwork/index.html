The weakest point of Your Roof.

Chimney stacks endure the worst of the Welsh weather. They are exposed to driving rain, freezing temperatures, and high winds from all four sides. Over time, the mortar pointing holding the bricks together begins to crack and crumble, acting like a sponge that draws water directly into your home.

Equally critical is the leadwork—the metal flashing that seals the joint between your chimney and the roof tiles. If this lead is aged, split, or incorrectly installed by a previous builder, water will pour straight down your chimney breast and ruin your interior plaster and decorations. At Eagle Eye Roofing, we fix the source, not just the symptom.

Our Expertise

CHIMNEY & LEAD SERVICES

◇

Chimney Repointing

We safely grind out the old, perished mortar from your brickwork and meticulously repoint the joints. We also secure loose chimney pots and install protective cowls to stop birds and rain from entering the flue.

◇

Chimney Removal

If your chimney is no longer in use, leaning dangerously, or causing constant damp, we offer full chimney stack removals. We dismantle it below the roofline, install fresh timbers, and tile over the gap so it blends seamlessly.

◇

Custom Lead Flashing

We are experts in traditional leadwork. From step flashings around chimneys to intricate lead valleys that carry heavy rainwater off your roof, we cut, boss, and weld lead to create a perfect, impenetrable seal.
