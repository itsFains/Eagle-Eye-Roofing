The hidden danger of Rotting Timber.

Your roofline (the fascias, soffits, and gutters) is your home's main defense against heavy Welsh rain. For decades, these boards were made of timber. Over time, constant exposure to moisture causes the paint to peel and the wood to rot—often acting like a sponge that transfers damp directly into your roof rafters and interior walls.

Many "cowboy" builders will simply nail cheap plastic boards directly over your rotting timber to hide the problem. This traps the moisture inside and accelerates structural failure. At Eagle Eye Roofing, we do it right. We completely strip away the old, rotten wood, treat the rafter ends, and install solid, high-grade UPVC that will protect your property for a lifetime.

Complete Roofline System

WHAT WE INSTALL

◇

UPVC Fascia Boards

The fascia is the long, straight board that runs along the lower edge of the roof. It supports the bottom row of roof tiles and holds the weight of your guttering (and the torrential rainwater inside it). We use heavy-duty, load-bearing UPVC to ensure it never bows or snaps.

◇

Ventilated Soffits

The soffit is the board tucked away underneath the fascia, visible when looking up at the roof. We install specialist ventilated soffits that allow a continuous flow of air into your roof space, stopping your loft timbers from sweating and rotting from the inside out.

◇

Deep-Flow Guttering

Standard gutters often overflow during heavy South Wales storms, pouring water directly down your brickwork. We install high-capacity, deep-flow UPVC guttering systems and secure downpipes that channel water swiftly and safely away from your home's foundations.
